## ✋ How Does It Work?

The magic happens in three simple steps:

1. **Your camera sees you** - GESTURE-FX activates your computer's webcam
2. **Your hands become the controller** - Hold up your hand in a specific shape (like a frame with your thumb and forefinger)
3. **Your picture transforms instantly** - The real-time image changes into a cartoon or glitch effect while you watch

It's powered by advanced hand-tracking technology called MediaPipe, which works entirely on your computer. This means your privacy stays protected because nothing ever leaves your device.

## 📥 Download and Installation Help

If you run into any trouble, here are common solutions:

### My browser blocks the download
Sometimes browsers show warnings for new apps. Look for a message saying "Keep" or "Allow" and click it. Your file is safe.

### The app doesn't open after downloading
Make sure you have a supported browser installed. Try Chrome or Edge if you're not sure. Also, disable any strict popup blockers temporarily.

### My camera doesn't work
Check that no other app is using your camera. Close Zoom, Teams, or any video calling software. Then refresh the GESTURE-FX page.

Adjust your lighting. Sit facing a window or lamp so your face is well-lit. The tracking works best with even light.

## 💡 Tips for Best Results

- **Lighting matters** - Bright, even lighting helps the hand tracking work better
- **Keep your hands visible** - Make sure your hands are in the camera frame
- **Move slowly** - Quick motions may confuse the tracking system
- **Test different distances** - Find the sweet spot where your hands and face are both clearly visible
- **Practice gestures** - It takes a few tries to perfect the finger-framing gesture
